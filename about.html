About Joyful Assembly

Our History, Mission, and Vision

Our History

Joyful Assembly Pentecostal Church was founded in 2020 with a vision to reach the lost and disciple believers. What started as a small prayer group of 5 members has grown into a thriving congregation of over 100 members. Through the years, we have witnessed countless testimonies of healing, restoration, and transformation.

Our Mission

To glorify God by making disciples through worship, evangelism, fellowship, and service, demonstrating the love of Christ to our community and the world.

Our Vision

To be a lighthouse of hope, raising kingdom-minded leaders and transforming communities through the power of the Holy Spirit.

What We Believe

We believe in the Trinity, the deity of Jesus Christ, the infallibility of Scripture, and the baptism of the Holy Spirit.

Our Core Values

Love, Integrity, Excellence, Faith, Unity, and Service are at the heart of everything we do.

Community Impact

Through outreach programs, food banks, and educational support, we serve our community with compassion.

Joyful Assembly

A Pentecostal church committed to spreading the Gospel and making disciples of all nations.
